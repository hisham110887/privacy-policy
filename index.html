Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Modern Compass. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle information when you use our mobile application.

Modern Compass provides Islamic utility features including Prayer Times, Qibla Compass, Digital Tasbih, Hijri Calendar, and Mosque Finder services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when necessary to provide and improve our services.

2.1 Location Information

Modern Compass may request location permission to provide location-based features such as:

  • Accurate prayer times
  • Nearby mosque search
  • Qibla direction calculation
  • Local Islamic services

Your location information is used only for providing these features. We do not sell your location data to third parties.

2.2 Device Information

We may collect limited device information such as device model, Android version, application version, and anonymous identifiers for improving app performance and security.

3. Firebase Services

Modern Compass uses Firebase services provided by Google to improve reliability and performance.

  • Firebase Analytics may collect anonymous usage information to understand app performance and user interactions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash reports and technical information to help us fix application problems.

These services do not collect personally identifiable information unless provided by the user.

4. Advertising

Modern Compass uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and interaction data to provide relevant advertisements.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices through Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Features and Data Usage

  • Prayer Times: Uses location information to calculate accurate prayer schedules.
  • Qibla Compass: Uses device sensors and location information to determine Qibla direction.
  • Mosque Finder: Uses location information to show nearby mosques.
  • Digital Tasbih: Counter data may be stored locally on your device.
  • Hijri Calendar: Provides Islamic date conversion features.

6. Permissions We Use

Modern Compass requests only the permissions required to provide its features. These permissions include:

  • Location Permission: Used for prayer time calculation, Qibla direction, and nearby mosque services.
  • Sensor Permission: Used by the compass feature to determine direction.
  • Internet Permission: Used for advertisements, updates, and online services.

You can manage or disable permissions anytime from your Android device settings. Some features may not work properly after disabling required permissions.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Modern Compass stores only the information necessary to provide application functionality.

  • Tasbih counter data may be stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash information may be stored by Firebase according to Google's retention policies.
  • Location data is processed only when required for location-based features.

We do not keep personal information longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Services

Modern Compass may use third-party services that process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase

  • Google Play Services

These third-party providers may collect information to provide, maintain, analyze, and improve their services.
